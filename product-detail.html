The Collection

The gemstone story

Citrine, quartz, and amethyst — natural gemstones, each with its own distinctive character and symbolic aura.

Citrine
Warmth · Optimism · Radiance
Citrine carries the warmth of the sun — a stone of optimism and radiant energy. Worn close, it invites light into every moment.
Quartz
Clarity · Harmony · Light
Clear and luminous, quartz is a stone of clarity and harmony. Its translucency catches the light in endlessly shifting, graceful ways.
Amethyst
Calm · Balance · Quiet Elegance
Amethyst speaks in whispers — a stone of calm and quiet elegance, bringing a sense of balance and poise to everything it adorns.
Gemstones vary by model. Each stone is individually selected and hand-set, making every piece uniquely yours.
